Greece · Zakynthos · One home

The Ionian.
A private path to the sea.

On the quiet western coast of Zakynthos — in a village that still belongs to its villagers — a single house opens directly onto the Ionian. Eight bedrooms, two infinity pools, olive and cypress at the door, and a stretch of beach below that fills with no other footprints. We chose this one home, alone, because it does what the rest of the island rarely lets you have: the beach, the privacy, the silence — all in the same week.

An eight-bedroom house on the quiet western coast, opening directly onto the sea. Two infinity pools draw a thin line between you and the Ionian. Two kitchens — one inside, one under vines — so a private chef can work without interrupting the rhythm of the house. Snorkel with sea turtles before breakfast; a short boat ride to the Blue Caves before lunch. The villa is wired discreetly — Bluetooth speakers tucked into the architecture, smart TVs hidden when you don't want them — but never feels engineered.
